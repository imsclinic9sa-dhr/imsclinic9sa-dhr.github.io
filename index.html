DHR Backup — Clinic 9 (Industrial Medical Services)

DHR Backup is used only by Clinic 9 staff. It copies the clinic's own Digital Health Record database to the clinic's Google Drive account (clinic9.backup@gmail.com) as a daily backup.

Privacy Policy

  • What the app accesses: only the files it creates itself in the folder "DHR Backups" (Google Drive scope "drive.file"). It cannot see, open or change any other file in Google Drive.
  • What is uploaded: an encrypted copy (AES-256) of the clinic's health record database. It can only be opened with the clinic's backup password, which is never sent to Google.
  • Who can access the backups: only authorised Clinic 9 personnel.
  • Data is not sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.
  • Personal and health information is handled in line with the Philippine Data Privacy Act of 2012 (RA 10173).
  • To stop access: remove "DHR Backup" at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions
  • Contact: clinic9.backup@gmail.com
