Our Privacy Commitment

DHR Backup is an internal application intended exclusively for authorized Clinic 9 personnel. Its purpose is to create secure backup copies of the clinic's Digital Health Record (DHR) database and store those backups in the clinic's designated Google Drive account.

1. Purpose of the Application

DHR Backup is designed to support the protection and continuity of Clinic 9's digital health records through scheduled or authorized database backup operations.

The application is used internally by Clinic 9 personnel. It is not intended for public use or for providing healthcare services directly to patients.

2. Information We Access and Process

DHR Backup processes the clinic's existing Digital Health Record database for backup purposes.

This may include personal and sensitive personal information contained in clinic records, such as:

  • Patient identification and contact information;
  • Medical and health-related records;
  • Consultation and treatment information; and
  • Other information maintained in the clinic database.

The application does not collect additional patient information for advertising, marketing, profiling, or unrelated purposes.

3. Google Drive Access and Permissions

DHR Backup uses Google Drive to store the clinic's database backup files in the designated Google account:

Google Account: ims.clinic9sa@gmail.com

The application requests the Google Drive drive.file permission. This permission allows the application to access files that it creates or that the user explicitly opens or selects through the application.

DHR Backup does not request unrestricted access to the user's entire Google Drive. It does not intentionally access unrelated personal files or documents.

Backup files are organized in a designated Google Drive folder named DHR Backups.

4. Data Storage and Encryption

The clinic's Digital Health Record database is prepared as a backup file and encrypted using AES-256 encryption before it is uploaded to Google Drive.

The backup password is maintained separately by authorized clinic personnel and is not transmitted to Google Drive as part of the backup file.

Access to the original database, backup files, and decryption credentials is restricted to authorized personnel in accordance with the clinic's internal procedures.

5. How We Use the Information

Information processed by DHR Backup is used exclusively for the following purposes:

  • Creating backup copies of the clinic's database;
  • Uploading encrypted backup files to the clinic's Google Drive;
  • Supporting database recovery when necessary; and
  • Maintaining the availability and continuity of clinic records.

Information is not used for advertising, marketing, commercial profiling, or unrelated purposes.

6. Data Sharing and Disclosure

DHR Backup does not sell, rent, or trade patient information. It does not share clinic records with third parties for advertising or independent commercial purposes.

Google Drive is used as the designated storage service for backup files. Google's processing of information is subject to its own applicable privacy policies and terms.

Information may be disclosed when required by applicable laws, regulations, or valid legal processes.

7. Data Retention and Deletion

Backup files are retained according to the clinic's internal data retention and backup management procedures.

Authorized Clinic 9 personnel are responsible for managing backup retention, reviewing stored backups, and deleting files when they are no longer required, subject to applicable legal and regulatory retention requirements.

8. Information Security

Clinic 9 applies administrative, technical, and organizational measures intended to protect personal and sensitive personal information against unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, loss, or destruction.

These measures include, as applicable:

  • Restricted access to authorized clinic personnel;
  • Encrypted database backup files;
  • Controlled access to backup credentials;
  • Use of the clinic's designated Google Drive account; and
  • Internal procedures for backup and recovery operations.

9. Philippine Data Privacy Act of 2012

Clinic 9 recognizes its responsibilities under the Philippine Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act No. 10173), its Implementing Rules and Regulations, and applicable National Privacy Commission issuances.

Personal information is handled according to applicable data protection principles, including transparency, legitimate purpose, and proportionality.

Individuals may exercise applicable data privacy rights, subject to legal limitations and the clinic's record retention obligations, by contacting the clinic through the contact information below.

10. Revoking Google Drive Access

The Google account owner may revoke DHR Backup's access through their Google Account permissions settings:

Manage Google Account App Permissions

Revoking access prevents the application from using the previously granted Google authorization. It does not automatically delete backup files already stored in Google Drive.

The latest version will be published on this page, together with its updated effective date.
